Vælg en ny adgangskode
======================

We removed the two-factor authentication, recovery codes and passkeys of your classbeat account and signed you out everywhere, as asked to restore your access. Choose a new password, then set them up again on your account page.

Hvis du vil vælge en ny adgangskode, skal du åbne linket nedenfor (gyldigt i 30 minutter). Når du nulstiller adgangskoden, bliver du logget ud overalt.

Vælg en ny adgangskode:
https://classbeat.example.test/da/reset#token=SAMPLE

If you did not ask for this, tell us right away. Kontakt os på support@classbeat.eu
